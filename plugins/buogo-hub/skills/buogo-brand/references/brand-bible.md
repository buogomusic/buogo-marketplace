# BUOGO Brand Bible — Condensed for Reel Generation

Source: BUOGO Brand Bible v1.0 (2025). This is the operating document for every Reel decision.

## Identity at a glance

- **Artist:** BUOGO (@buogomusic)
- **Genre:** Tech house
- **Base:** Paranavaí, PR, Brazil → World
- **DAW:** Ableton Live
- **Horizon (5 years):** Coachella, EXIT, Tomorrowland, EDC
- **Commitment:** 12 months minimum on this direction, no changes

## Purpose

To play the world's biggest festivals. To be recognized as one of the most respected and relevant producers in global tech house. Long-term creative legacy + real music business with international shows and financial freedom — both coexist without contradiction. **Production is the core engine. Always has been. Always will be.**

## Archetype split — apply to every Reel

### 80% OUTLAW
The rebel. Goes against the system, against mediocrity, against the low standard of the scene. Doesn't attack people — attacks conformism. Has something to rebel against and makes it clear without saying it directly. **The attitude speaks for itself.**

### 20% JESTER
Irreverent, not a clown. Lightness lives in the character, not in exaggeration. Doesn't need to be funny to be funny — attitude already communicates. **Reference: Tyler the Creator, not Dillon Francis.** The 20% Jester creates the human differentiator: moments nobody expected from a Tech House producer, without losing authority.

> **Master rule:** Buogo doesn't explain what he is. He shows it.

## The film — narrative

Every artist with a strong brand is a character inside a film. Content is scenes. Every post, interview, visual, release — a scene that advances the plot.

**Plot:** Rags to Riches — Rise against time.

Someone who came from an invisible place and is building the path to where few arrive. Not a story of comfort — a story of **obsession with standard and refusal to accept the average level**.

**Elevator pitch:** Buogo is a tech house producer from the Brazilian interior who refuses mediocrity and builds, with consistency and high standards, the path to the world's biggest stages.

## Quality filter — apply before posting anything

> **If any other producer could have made it — don't post it.**
> There is no generic content. There is Buogo content or there is nothing.

## Visual identity

### Color palette
- `#080808` — Black (primary background)
- `#1A1A1A` — Dark gray (secondary background)
- `#F0EDE8` — Off-white (primary text)
- `#FF3C00` — Accent orange (highlights only — sparingly)

### Typography
- **Bebas Neue** — Titles, artist name, headlines. Always uppercase.
- **IBM Plex Mono** — Labels, tags, dates, metadata. Precise, intentional.
- **IBM Plex Sans** — Body text, long descriptions. 300/400 for reading, 500 for emphasis.

Never mix more than two fonts in the same piece. Default: Bebas Neue + IBM Plex Mono.

### Photography & video
- Direct flash. High contrast. Symmetric composition.
- No soft filters, no warm environments.
- Edit preset: Contrast +60 / Saturation -40 / Blacks -30 / Whites +20 / Exposure -0.3 / Grain 40
- Lighting: low key, single light source, cold blues, blackout curtains, monitor glow
- Visual register: Fight Club / Breaking Bad / Dark / Interstellar

### Wardrobe
Solid color athletic sets. No busy prints. No unnecessary visual information. Clothes don't compete with the character.

## Tone of voice — does and doesn't

### DOES
- Straight to the point. No beating around the bush.
- Silence is worth more than empty content.
- Confidence without arrogance.
- Talks about process, not results.
- Consistent posture — online and offline.

### DOESN'T
- Doesn't attack other artists.
- Doesn't over-explain. Doesn't justify himself.
- Doesn't post under pressure of frequency.
- Doesn't use empty hype language.
- Doesn't follow trends without filtering.

## Narrative & aesthetic references

- **Fight Club** — Identity built against the system. Rupture with the standard. Narrative intensity.
- **Breaking Bad** — Character transformation with clear purpose. Every action has weight.
- **Dark** — Cold and precise aesthetic. Visual density. Those paying attention understand.
- **Interstellar** — Scale. Boundless ambition. Greatness as natural goal, not exception.

## Content pillars — every Reel belongs to one

### Pillar 01 — RELEASE WITH A PLOT
Buogo appears. The release is scripted like a scene from the film. Not just dropping music — creating a moment with beginning, middle and impact. **Character at the center.**
→ Release video, music video, cover visual, campaign

### Pillar 02 — PROCESS / DAW / STUDIO
Shows the real work. The DAW, the production process, the technical behind-the-scenes. **Proves the level is high without saying it's high.**
→ Session clip, track breakdown, making of

### Pillar 03 — CHARACTER / OPINION
Posture, worldview, thoughts on the scene, on production, on standards. **Humanizes without losing posture. Outlaw shows up most here.**
→ Talking head, opinion piece, direct statement
